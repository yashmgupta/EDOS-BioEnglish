# 258201 · Communicative English for Academic Analysis in Biology

Naresuan University, course 258201, semester 1/2569. Course plan pp. 8–12. Assessment plan p. 14. The syllabus groups weeks 4–5, 10–11 and 12–13. Their separate weekly tasks below are suggested teaching choices.

## Week 1 · Course orientation and academic integrity

Show students where to find your course rules. Ask them to use the AI disclosure format accepted by their lecturer.

Related chapter: 258201-01.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-01.pptx

## Week 2 · Article structure, database searching and pretest

Use the approved pretest before practice (5% in the assessment plan). Pair chapter 02 with chapter 03 for the database workshop. Use the two fictional practice records on the slides for offline practice.

Related chapter: 258201-02.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-02.pptx

## Week 3 · Skimming, scanning and close reading

Ask students to skim silently, then scan for one detail and close-read a limitation. Transfer the same three steps to a supplied article abstract.

Related chapter: 258201-04.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-03.pptx

## Week 4 · Paraphrasing without copying

First part of the shared weeks 4–5 topic. Use a short sentence before students work with an article paragraph.

Related chapter: 258201-05.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-04.pptx

## Week 5 · Summarizing a biology paragraph

Second part of the shared weeks 4–5 topic. For peer feedback, ask students to point to the main idea and one detail they chose to leave out.

Related chapter: 258201-05.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-05.pptx

## Week 6 · Analyzing introductions and methods

Use two short introductions. Students identify what is known, the gap, the aim and how each author gathered evidence. Show chapter 02 for section names.

Related chapter: 258201-04.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-06.pptx

## Week 7 · Analyzing results and discussion

Teach the difference between a measured result and an interpretation. Use the prepared catalase table activity in chapter 06 for further practice.

Related chapter: 258201-06.

PowerPoint: weeks/Week-07.pptx

## Week 8 · Midterm examination

Midterm assessment (15%). The syllabus specifies article structure, a summary and interpretation of a figure or table. Use a lecturer-selected assessment article. The following example is for preparation.

## Week 9 · Writing conclusions and abstracts

Use the same findings twice. Students first write a conclusion, then place it in a short structured and unstructured abstract.

First part of the shared weeks 10–11 topic. Have each student plan an introduction, a body paragraph and a conclusion before drafting.

Second part of the shared weeks 10–11 topic. Ask students to highlight evidence and analysis in different ways, then revise one transition.

First part of the shared weeks 12–13 topic. Use short extracts and a comparison table with one row per source.

## Week 13 · Writing a synthesis paragraph

Second part of the shared weeks 12–13 topic. Students turn a comparison table into one paragraph linking both sources. The chapter 07 activity provides a longer task.

## Week 14 · Language for academic discussion

Run a short Q&A roleplay. Partners should listen for a clear claim, a question about evidence and a polite answer.

## Week 15 · Oral presentation skills in biology

Use the syllabus two-minute mock presentation. Ask the listener to identify the research question and one result without looking at the notes.

## Week 16 · Group oral presentation and course reflection

The syllabus calls for an 8–10 minute group presentation on a selected article, Q&A and reflection. Oral presentation is 10% and peer assessment is 5% in the published assessment plan. Use the lecturer’s approved rubric.
