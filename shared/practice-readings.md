# Short practice readings for biology English

These are fictional classroom records written for the studio. The numbers are simulated. Use them for reading, discussion and rehearsal. For assignments requiring a published article, use the article selected by the student or lecturer.

## Record A · Leaf width on two paths

EDOS class observation. Practice research report. Classroom record A.

The class asked whether leaf width differed between two paths. On one morning, students measured ten leaves from one shrub on a shaded path and ten leaves from one shrub on an open path. The mean width was 6 cm on the shaded path and 4 cm on the open path. The class observed two shrubs. A difference between these shrubs does not show that shade caused the difference.

## Record B · Reading about leaves and light

EDOS class reading group. Practice review. Classroom record B.

The group read three classroom reports about leaves and light. It compared how the writers described shade and measured leaf size. Two reports measured width. One measured length. The group suggested checking the part measured before comparing numbers across reports. It collected no new measurements.

## Classroom prompts

- Search skills: Which record has original observations? Which one reviews earlier reports? Give a phrase as evidence.
- Reading: Skim Record A for the question. Scan it for one number. Read closely to find one limit.
- Writing: Report the two means in one sentence. Then write a separate sentence explaining what the means can tell you.
- Presenting: Explain the result in 30 seconds. Ask a partner to tell you one number and one limit they heard.
