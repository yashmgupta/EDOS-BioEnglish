# Lecturer guide to the 258201 teaching archive

The strongest first use of this collection is a short lesson on writing about results. The Week 7 slides give students a writing sequence. Paper 3 gives them a table they can actually read. In the revised activity, students can support a sentence with numbers, then explain what the table leaves open.

## Which files belong with each studio lesson?

The IDs below are studio chapter numbers. The week numbers in the archive refer to the original teaching schedule.

## File selection notes

The outer ZIP contains 118 files, including 46 PNG images, 28 PDFs, 22 Word files, 15 PowerPoint decks, three HTML activities, three nested ZIPs and one notebook. These counts describe files. A PDF may be the print edition of a Word document or slide deck.

- The eight files inside the Week 3 ZIP also appear byte for byte in the expanded Week 3 folder. The two similarly named reading-exercise Word files at the outer level and inside that folder have different text. Choose the required version before assigning it.
- The file labeled as registrar workload is an exact copy of the reading-practice PDF. Two decks inside the Week 5 folder carry different week numbers but have the same editable slide text about active and passive voice.
- The sticker ZIP contains 12 PNGs for the results and discussion lesson. Use the editable teaching deck for changes to wording and examples.
- Both paragraph-swap Word packets include lecturer answers after the student pages. Export the activity pages separately before sharing. The four-paper answer key also belongs with lecturer materials.
- The nested submission archive includes 11 individually identified PDFs. Leave those files out of student downloads, course repositories and AI packs. The research notebook and QR image have no role in the selected language activity.

The exercise key labels the numerical data in the four paper exercises as simulated. For the catalase activity, keep the units and observation period with the numbers. Ask for the highest mean **among the pH values tested**. The table cannot identify the exact pH of peak activity between two tested values.
